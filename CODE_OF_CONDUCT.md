# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Participate constructively, respect privacy and consent, give actionable technical feedback, and assume good intent without dismissing reported harm.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, publication of private data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Raise conduct concerns privately with the repository maintainer, avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal information. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or block accounts in proportion to the behavior. Reports should be handled discreetly and without retaliation; no absolute confidentiality guarantee is made.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
